Everything a small service business needs

From the first price to the first booking.

Government case analysis

Search Taiwan's Government e-Procurement System inside the app: award amounts, competitors and their win records, with a local AI summary and Google AI Mode lookups.

Service pricing calculator

Enter your hourly pay, fixed costs and target profit. Pricing AI works out the real cost of every service and the price that reaches your margin, with tax and rounding for 14 currencies.

Booking website builder

Start from a clean template in English, 繁體中文, 日本語, Português or Español. Edit visually, add a FAQ, flow charts and a dark high-tech look. Export a ZIP or deploy to Firebase.

Bookings in Google Forms and Calendar

One Apps Script creates the booking form and a calendar that shows busy times publicly. Every booking becomes a calendar event, and the site shows availability.

Local AI on Mac and Windows

The desktop app runs open models on your own GPU: it writes your site from a chat, rewrites parts on request and can draw banner pictures. Nothing is sent to a cloud AI.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the app and the browser version?

The desktop app adds local AI, government case analysis, competitor lookups and one-click deploy. The browser version is free and needs no install, but has no AI.

Chrome offers to install Pricing AI. Is that the app?

No. Chrome installs the browser version as a shortcut called Pricing AI Web. For AI and case analysis, download the Mac or Windows app from this page.

Is Pricing AI open source?

Yes. The code is on GitHub under the Apache License 2.0, together with the installers in Releases and the licenses of all third-party libraries.

Is it free?

The web version is free to use: the price calculator, the site builder and ZIP export. The Mac and Windows apps add local AI and one-click deploy.

Does the AI send my data to the cloud?

No. In the desktop app the AI models run on your own computer. The web version has no AI and keeps your data in your browser.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You edit the page visually or by chat, and the booking form connects through a Google Form.

Which languages are supported?

The app and the site templates come in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish.

How do I receive bookings?

Visitors fill in your Google Form from your site. A script adds each booking to a Google Calendar, and you can turn on email notifications in Google Forms.
